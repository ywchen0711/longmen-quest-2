# 龍門刀客傳 貳 · 萬妖鈴 Longmen Quest II: The Bell of Ten Thousand Beasts

The sequel to 龍門刀客傳 Longmen Quest: an original 8-bit wuxia party RPG in a pop-up storybook, about the size of
the 8-bit and 16-bit console RPGs that let monsters join your party. It is a side story of 《龍門雙奕》 (the Longmen Twins).

- The same pop-up book as the first game (three.js): the world is an open book on a wooden table; trees, houses,
  people and beasts are die-cut cards that fold up as you come near; map changes turn the page; menus are paper notes.
- Famicom-style 8-bit art in NES (2C02) colours, an original NES-synth score, bilingual (繁體中文 / English).
- Up to four fight together: 奕叡, the companions who join through the story, and monsters that ask to come along.

Play: https://ywchen0711.github.io/longmen-quest-2/

## The story (four chapters, an ending and an epilogue)

Half a year after Tu Qianqiu fell, a strange bell starts to ring, and with every peal the creatures of the land go
mad. 奕叡 sets out again, and this time his little brother 奕碩 insists on coming.

1. **鈴聲 The Bell.** Longmen, Xinghua Village and the Hundred Beasts Camp, where the glutton 牛飽飽 takes in
   creatures that come to their senses. A bell-bound tiger on the Wolf Slope; the timid escort 武大膽 hiding in a wine
   jar; the rival 楚天闊 on the Luoxia arena; the Abbot 苦禪 and a shard of the Bell of Ten Thousand Beasts. The Bell
   Temple has been taken by the 馭妖宗 (Beastbinder Sect); its envoy names their master, 墨無涯, and the bell's three
   souls (water, fire, ice).
2. **水鄉 The Water Country.** Across the mended bridge: the canal city of Suhe, the couplet contest of 文對仗, the
   free clinic of the forgetful Doctor 莫記得, 柳寒煙's news, and the old Su mansion where 蘇小婉 pours tea every night,
   not knowing she died twenty years ago. A bell-bound river jiao guards the Water Soul; the Ship King lends his ship.
3. **南疆 The Southlands.** By sea: Fishing-fire Isle, the Miao village of 阿蠻 (who talks only to beasts), the
   Bamboo Sea, Poison Dragon Marsh and the volcano. In the Red Flame Caves 楚天闊 learns that the sect master is his
   own father, and joins you. With two souls in hand you rest at the island inn... and the doctor who "forgets
   everything" drugs the soup and steals them. 鐵無私 of the Alliance lowers the harbour chain of the north.
4. **萬妖峰 The Peak of Ten Thousand Beasts.** Beiyan Pass, the Snow Wolf camp, the Ice Soul Caves, and the Tower of
   Ten Thousand Beasts, where 冷笑天 holds the gate. At the top, the twist: 墨無涯 is 小婉's brother, binding every beast
   so that none can ever kill again, and hoping the bell will call her spirit back. She was beside you all along.

The epilogue tells what became of everyone, and ends, as it should, in the Listening Rain House.

Twists: the forgetful doctor remembers everything; the rival is the villain's son; the villain is the ghost's
brother; and the fortune-teller who is always wrong is right exactly once.

## The people (噱頭人物)

- **胡半仙** Hu Banxian: a fortune-teller whose banner says "no charge if I'm wrong". Every prophecy is the opposite of
  what happens (read them backwards: they are hints).
- **賈八方** Jia Bafang: a peddler who sells everything, his own shoes first, then his hat, then his coat. By the
  north he is wearing a barrel. (His own clothes are real gear in his stall.)
- **武大膽** Wu Dadan ("Great Courage"): an eight-foot bearded escort who hides in wine jars. Party tank: 掩護 Cover,
  怒吼 Roar and 裝死 Play Dead.
- **文對仗** Wen Duizhang: a scholar who speaks only in couplets; you win him with a couplet contest. Party caster
  (ink arts, Thunderclap, Word of Stillness).
- **蘇小婉** Su Xiaowan: a ghost who doesn't know she is dead (claws pass through her: she slips more blows). Party
  healer (Spring Rain, Call Back).
- **楚天闊** Chu Tiankuo: the rival who challenges you in every town and writes each defeat in a little book. Joins in
  chapter 3 (天闊一劍).
- **牛飽飽** Niu Baobao: the glutton who runs the beast camp; he gives you four buns and you get three. His cousins at
  every inn swap your party by pigeon post.
- **莫記得** Mo Jide: the doctor who forgets everything (he doesn't).
- **霍天下** Huo Tianxia: a braggart who claims every deed you do, and keeps getting tied up.
- **阿蠻** A-Man: a Miao girl raised by wolves; she talks to beasts, and your creatures translate.
- **墨無涯** Mo Wuya: the master of the Beastbinder Sect.
- Returning: 奕叡, 奕碩 (party), 白先生 (storyteller and save point), 柳寒煙, 苦禪, 冷笑天, 鐵無私.

## How it plays

**Party battles (front view)**
- Up to four in battle against groups of up to four foes. The foes stand on the page as die-cut cards; the party
  stands in the foreground as cards seen from behind (people) or as their field sprites (monsters).
- Commands per member: 攻擊 Attack (choose a target), 武功/絕技 Arts, 道具 Items, 防禦 Defend, 逃走 Run. B goes back to
  the previous member. Then everyone acts by agility.
- Target marks blink round the card being hit; blows land on the right card (claws, jaws, arrows, magic, a kicked
  ball, ink, a saber swing); damage tags pop over that card. A fallen member's card lies flat until revived.
- Arts: single and all-foe damage, single and party healing, revival, cure, sleep (one or all), seal, guard, party
  guard, cover, roar (draw the blows), cheer (party attack up), pick-up (steal), play dead, and more.
- Bosses use only what anyone can: stats, gear-like numbers, the shared arts, escorts in their formation, salves and a
  smarter AI. Enemies never scale with your level.

**魔物加入 Monsters that join**
- After a won battle, the last foe beaten may get up and ask to come along. The chance depends on its kind (common
  creatures often, big beasts rarely) and on its hidden heart: throw it 肉包 Meat Buns or 靈果 Spirit Fruit during the
  battle to warm it to you. Charm (親和) gear such as the 牧童短笛 flute, the 百花香囊 sachet and A-Man's bone whistle
  helps too. Up to three of each kind.
- You name it (keep its kind's name, pick a suggestion or type your own; rename later at the camp).
- It joins the party if there is room, otherwise it goes to the 百獸山寨 Hundred Beasts Camp, where your resting
  creatures wander the pens. 牛飽飽 (or his cousins at any inn) swaps the party, renames and sets creatures free.
- Monsters level up on their own growth curve (by role: tank, healer, caster, spirit, quick, brute, balanced), learn
  skills at set levels, have a level cap by kind, and wear a 項圈 collar and a 佩飾 pendant.
- 66 kinds can join: 64 common foes and big beasts of the gallery (including 22 new to this game) and the money toads.
  The bestiary marks every kind that has joined you (★) and shows each kind's role.

**Gear, drops, the paper doll**
- People wear seven pieces (weapon, head, armour, cloak, bracer, pendant, boots); each wields its own weapon class
  (奕叡 blades, 奕碩 balls, 武大膽 clubs, 文對仗 brushes, 蘇小婉 fans, 楚天闊 swords). 奕叡's paper doll shows what he wears
  on the map, on his battle card and in his portrait. Shops compare a piece for whoever you pick.
- Every foe has drops; bosses always leave their signature piece.

**The world**
- 11 towns and villages (龍門鎮, 杏花村, 百獸山寨, 落霞鎮, 蘇河城, 望月渡, 漁火島, 青竹寨, 焰口村, 北雁關, 雪狼部) and
  9 dungeons (野狼坡, 鈴音寺, 蘇家舊宅, 碧波洞, 竹海 (optional), 毒龍沼, 赤焰窟, 冰魄窟, 萬妖塔): 37 maps.
- Gates: the Broken Bridge, the ship (sail the seas, land on any shore), the harbour chain of Beiyan Pass, the ice wall.
- Save with 白先生, at any inn, or anywhere from the menu; autosave on pagehide and every 30 s.

**Controls**: arrows/WASD, Z/Enter/Space = A, X/Esc/Backspace = B; gamepads; on touch screens a d-pad and A/B beside
the picture in landscape and under it in portrait. iPhone/iPad: safe areas, `100dvh`, audio unlocked on the first
tap, `navigator.audioSession`, AAC music.

## Speed

The same budget as the first game: one instanced draw for all pop-ups of a map, one baked ground texture, no shadow
maps or lights, one post pass; no map drawing behind menus or still message boxes, 30 fps when standing still, the
3D view capped at 720 px (680 on phones), and a watchdog that steps quality down. Followers are three more cards.

## Files

- `index.html`, `style.css`, `js/`:
  - `main.js` boot, title, loop, layout; `game.js` the party, followers, ship, encounters, recruiting, the camp,
    menus, shops, inns, saves; `battle.js` party battles; `rules.js` the shared rules; `data.js` people, arts, items,
    foes, species, formations; `story.js` the opening, the finale and the epilogue
  - `maps.js` + `maps_base.js` + `maps_ch1.js` ... `maps_ch4.js` the places and their people; `story_lines.js` the
    recurring characters' lines; `world_map.js` (generated)
  - `view.js`, `tiles.js`, `paper.js` the pop-up book; `doll.js` the paper doll; `ui.js`, `input.js`, `audio.js`
- `tools/` (Python 3 + Pillow, numpy, scipy, ffmpeg): `world.py` (the world and its zones, with a reachability check
  per story stage), `gallery.py` (foe art from the Character Gallery), `people.py` (people from the gallery),
  `doll.py`, `music.py` (the score).

All new people and creatures were drawn for the Character Gallery (`C:\CharacterGallery`, `nes` style, groups
`longmen2` and `longmen2Foes`) and are pulled in by the tools.
